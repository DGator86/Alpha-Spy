# Alpha/Beta V2 Architecture Backtest — 2026-08-27

## Status

**FAIL — improved materially versus Blind V1, but not profitable or robust enough for promotion.**

This is a causal architecture replay over the available July 27–August 26 SPY tape. It is not an exact historical options-chain replay because the complete historical 0DTE chains were not captured during those sessions. V2 forward chain capture is intended to remove that limitation going forward.

## Test contract

- 23 sessions / 1,426 five-minute observations.
- Beta V2 logic: maturity-delayed 5m/15m/30m magnitude, conditional-direction and expected-absolute-move heads; signed validation; trust compressor; no future outcome is used before maturity.
- One trade maximum per day.
- Entry is the **first** five-minute timestamp where Beta is eligible and Alpha finds a structure clearing all gates; no best-time-of-day hindsight selection.
- Beta minimum trust: 0.25; Beta must mark the opportunity eligible.
- Alpha physical-distribution base: causal walk-forward standardized Ridge proxy on already-matured 15-minute tape outcomes.
- Beta prior blend uses the V2 trust/agreement weighting and 45% maximum authority cap; Q is not directionally overridden by Beta.
- Full bounded-risk geometry search: 47 named families, cheap-screened before full valuation.
- Risk cap: <= $100 modeled maximum loss, one structure unit.
- V2 candidate gates: forecast EV >= $3, probability of profit >= 0.55, execution-drag/risk <= 0.20, edge/execution-drag >= 1.25.
- Historical option prices remain synthetic Black-Scholes because the complete historical chains do not exist. Bid/ask, OI, volume and displayed depth are therefore modeled for this replay.

## What improved

The Beta prior materially improved the same architecture versus Alpha-only on the 13% IV replay:

- Alpha-only: -$38.65
- Beta+Alpha: -$9.67
- Improvement: +$28.98

Relative to the locked Blind V1 13%/1c result (-$55.60), V2 improved by +$45.93.

That is meaningful architectural progress, but it is not positive expectancy.

## Failure signatures

1. **The full optimizer repeatedly converges on call Christmas trees.** This is not automatically wrong — the payoff is a bounded target/range geometry, not simply a long-call bet — but the concentration means the optimizer is finding one synthetic-pricing niche rather than demonstrating broad family robustness.
2. **Forecast EV is materially too optimistic versus realized P&L.** Several trades forecast $20–$54 of EV yet realized small losses. That indicates remaining distribution/valuation miscalibration, not merely bad direction calls.
3. **Execution stress does not rescue the result.** Doubling modeled bid/ask spread reduced trades from 4 to 3 and produced -$13.82.
4. **IV sensitivity remains negative.** 13%, 20% and 30% synthetic-IV assumptions all produced negative total P&L.
5. **Beta improves selection but does not create a profitable allocator yet.** It filters/times Alpha better, but the combined P distribution still overstates the economic value of selected structures.

## Decision

Do **not** promote V2 to live capital from this test.

Keep the new architecture because it is clearly superior to V1, but the next attack should target **P-distribution calibration and forecast-EV realism**, not add more payoff families. The 47-family search is no longer the bottleneck.

Forward V2 sessions must capture the actual complete 0DTE option chain so the next blind replay can eliminate synthetic chain/spread assumptions and measure oracle regret, candidate EV calibration, fill drag, and realized P&L from the exact market surface observed by the engine.
